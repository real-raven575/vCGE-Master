    Unlock with

    Session PIN: not set Session PIN4 digits, forgotten when you leave. Turn on to lock the card instead of wiping it when you step away.
    Face ID / fingerprint: off Face or fingerprintTurn on to unlock with your face or fingerprint at the lock screen.

    Turning it on makes your browser save a passkey for this site on your device, because the browser requires it to check your face or fingerprint. The passkey holds no contact details. vCGE forgets it when the card is wiped, so after a wipe you turn it on again. Old ones can be deleted in your password manager.

    Auto lock after

    With a PIN, the card locks after 2 minutes without activity. That time is fixed.

    Wipe after lock

    Activity

      vCGE saves none of your card. Leaving, closing or reloading the page wipes it. With a 4-digit PIN set, the screen turning off or 2 idle minutes lock it instead, and 7 minutes after the lock it is wiped. Without a PIN, 30 s out of view or 2 idle minutes wipe it. The wipe asks first, and you hold Yes to confirm.
