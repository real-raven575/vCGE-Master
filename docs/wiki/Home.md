# vCGE wiki

**vCGE (vCard Generator Engine)** turns a photo of a business card into a phone contact, entirely on the device: no internet, nothing stored, and data leaves only when you share it. *Operation: Never Lose Contact.*

## 📲 Get vCGE
- **Free beta (0.8.1):** [real-raven575.github.io/vCGE-Master](https://real-raven575.github.io/vCGE-Master/). Its future home, vcge.app, is not live yet.
- **Google Play, coming first at $0.99.** It starts with a closed test. To be a tester, ask to join the [vcge-testers Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/vcge-testers).
- **Microsoft Store**, also $0.99, is planned after Google Play.
- The source code stays free and open. The store builds cost $0.99 once, with no ads and no subscription.
