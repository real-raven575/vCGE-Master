Getting vCGE free with your code

Thank you for testing vCGE! Your code makes the app free for you, so you never pay the $0.99. This takes about two minutes. Take it one step at a time, and you can't break anything.

The quick way: one tap

Your email has a red button that says Get vCGE free. Open the email on your Android phone and tap that button. Google Play opens with your code already filled in. Tap Redeem, then Install. That's it!

If the button doesn't work, or you'd rather go slowly, follow the steps below. They do the same thing.

Step by step

  1. Use your Android phone

    Do this on the Android phone you'll test on. Make sure the Play Store is signed in with the same Google account you used to join the tester group.

    To check: open the Play Store app and look at the little round picture at the top right. Tap it to see which account it is, and switch if you need to.

  2. Open your code page in Play

    In the Play Store app, tap your round picture (top right), then tap these, one after another:

    1. Payments & subscriptions
    2. Redeem code

    Or tap this button on your phone to go straight there:

    Open Redeem code

  3. Type your code and tap Redeem

    Your code is in your email. It looks like this, with your own letters and numbers:

    ABCD1234EFGH5678

    Type or paste it into the box, then tap Redeem. Each code works once, so it's yours alone.

  4. See "Free", then tap Install

    Play shows vCGE with the $0.99 crossed out and the word Free. Tap Install. You won't be charged.

    Don't see vCGE? Open the vCGE page in Play with this button:

    Open vCGE on Google Play

    If it still asks you to pay $0.99, stop. Don't pay. Just reply to the email and we'll sort it out.

  5. Open vCGE and keep it for 14 days

    Tap Open, try it on a business card or two, and keep vCGE on your phone for 14 days. Open it now and then. That's how you help Google say yes.

    Something confusing or broken? Just reply to the email and tell us. Every note helps.

If something gets stuck

  • "Item not found" or no Install button: the Play Store is probably on a different Google account. Go back to step 1 and switch to the account you joined the group with.
  • "This code has already been used": reply to the email and we'll send a fresh one.
  • On an iPhone or iPad: the Play test is Android only. You can try the free web version instead: real-raven575.github.io/vCGE-Master
  • Still stuck? Reply to the email. A real person reads every reply.
