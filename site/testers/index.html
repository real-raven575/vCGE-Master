Become a vCGE tester

vCGE turns a photo of a printed business card into a finished phone contact. The card is read on your phone, with no account and no internet. Before vCGE opens on Google Play, it needs a small group of Android testers to try it for two weeks. Thank you for helping.

  1. Join the tester group

    Ask to join with the Google account you use on your Android phone. That account is the one Google Play lets in.

    Open the tester group

  2. Opt in on Google Play

    Once you are in the group, open the testing link on the same account, tap Become a tester, then install vCGE from Google Play.

    Open the Play testing link

  3. The $0.99 is covered

    vCGE costs $0.99 on Google Play. Testers in the group get a free Google Play code to cover it, so testing costs you nothing.

  4. Keep it for 14 days

    Google asks that testers keep the app installed for 14 days. Scan a card now and then, and tell us in the group what worked and what did not.

Got your code?

Your code makes vCGE free on Google Play. The guide walks you through it, one step at a time.

Got your code? How to use it

Please keep it private

When you share a problem, describe it in words or use a made-up card. Please never post a photo of a real business card or anyone's real contact details.
